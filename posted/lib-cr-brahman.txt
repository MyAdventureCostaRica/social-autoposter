On 25 July 1824, an open council in Nicoya voted to join Costa Rica. Only 40 people attended the meeting to decide the future of the region.

Of the 25 individuals who signed the official act, 11 belonged to one family, the Viales Briceño. The province adopted a motto meaning of the homeland by our own will, and the date remains a national holiday today.

This landscape near Tilarán reflects the ranching culture that developed here over time. Local traditions like topes and bull riding persist in the towns of this province. The Brahman breed is common across these warm lowlands, grazing where the forest meets the pasture.
