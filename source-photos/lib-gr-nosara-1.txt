The Gravel Expedition (Episode 02, gravel cycling) · Day 4 of 9 · The wild coast. · Santa Teresa → Nosara
101.1 km · +1,530 m · meals: Breakfast · lodge tonight: Sendero Hotel & Villas, Nosara
The big traverse, and the wildest stretch of the trip — ridden in full, one hotel to the next. North out of Santa Teresa through Manzanillo, then onto the undeveloped middle coast of Nicoya: San Francisco de Coyote, Playa Coyote, Bejuco, Playa San Miguel — beaches most travellers never reach.
The line passes the Camaronal refuge, a nesting beach for several of the world's sea-turtle species, and Punta Islita, a tiny village known for the open-air art museum set into its streets, before running on past Garza to Nosara. A hundred kilometres of dirt and gravel along a coast defined by what it lacks — no resort strip, no traffic, river mouths instead of bridges. The endurance day, finished in the Blue Zone.
The Gravel Expedition (Episode 02, gravel cycling) · Day 5 of 9 · Seventy kilometres, straight up. · Nosara Loop · Queen Stage
69.3 km · +2,632 m · meals: Breakfast · lodge tonight: Sendero Hotel & Villas, Nosara
The signature day, and where all the climbing lives — 2,632 metres of it. North along the coast to Ostional, then a hard turn inland and up into the mountains east of town, a high point around 950 m, ridges through the highlands, and a long descent back to the sea and home to Nosara.
Ostional protects one of the planet's most important olive-ridley nesting beaches — the coast famous for the arribada, the mass arrival of nesting turtles. The hardest sustained climbing of the expedition, the Pacific at the bottom of the day and at the top of it. E-bike upgrade available on request.
Highlight of The Gravel Expedition (Episode 02, gravel cycling): The Nosara queen stage. — Seventy kilometres and 2,600 metres of climbing in one loop — the turtle coast at the bottom, a high point around 950 m in the middle.
Highlight of The Gravel Expedition (Episode 02, gravel cycling): The Ostional refuge. — The route skirts one of the planet's most important sea-turtle nesting beaches — the coast made famous by the arribada.
Lodge on The Gravel Expedition (Episode 02, gravel cycling): Sendero Hotel & Villas (Nosara) — Two nights in the Blue Zone. Rooms and villas tucked into a jungle preserve, a short path from the break at Playa Guiones.
