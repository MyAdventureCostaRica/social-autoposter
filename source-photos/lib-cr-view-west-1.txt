KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- On the Monteverde ridge, moisture from the Caribbean meets the Pacific winds. [source: https://www.discoverwildlife.com/environment/monteverde-cloud-forest-preserve]
- The northeast trade winds cross the continental divide at Monteverde; the cloud they carry feeds the cloud forest, which is why the ridge is so often in fog while the Pacific side below stays clearer. [source: earlier library note]
