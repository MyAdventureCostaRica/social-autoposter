The Hiking Expedition (Episode 03, hiking) · Day 7 of 9 · The mountain meets the sea. · The Pacific
meals: Breakfast, picnic lunch, celebration dinner · lodge tonight: Hotel Parador, Manuel Antonio
The last walking day. The valley finally lets go: the trail leaves the jungle for the coastal plain, palms replace oaks, and the air turns to salt. Ahead is Playa El Rey — a wild stretch of Pacific sand between the mouths of the Naranjo and the Savegre, reached by no paved road, shared with almost no one. Nine days ago you were standing in the páramo; the same watershed delivers you to the tide line.
There is time to stand in it properly — photographs, a toast, bare feet. Then the support team carries you north to Hotel Parador on the Manuel Antonio coast: five hectares of rainforest above the Pacific, cliff-top pools, and a celebration dinner that needs no speech.
Highlight of The Hiking Expedition (Episode 03, hiking): A sea of clouds. — Dawn from the high ridge, before the descent begins — the Pacific invisible below a white ocean that burns off by nine.
