PHOTO: Green hills of the Cordillera de Tilarán rolling away to the north, Arenal Volcano small on the horizon, cumulus overhead. Esteban's own photo, April 2026.
PLACE: The Cordillera de Tilarán above Tilarán, Guanacaste.
KNOWN FACTS: The Cordillera de Tilarán is the mountain range that carries Monteverde's cloud forest. Lake Arenal is the largest lake in Costa Rica, a reservoir formed by the Arenal dam that feeds hydroelectric plants. Arenal Volcano erupted violently in 1968 and stayed active until 2010.
