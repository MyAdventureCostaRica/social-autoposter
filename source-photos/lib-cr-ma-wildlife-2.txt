PLACE: Manuel Antonio National Park, central Pacific.
KNOWN FACTS: Costa Rica has 4 monkey species: the mantled howler, the white-faced capuchin, the Central American spider monkey and the Central American squirrel monkey. Two sloth species live in Costa Rica: the brown-throated three-toed sloth and Hoffmann's two-toed sloth. The snake's species is not identified: do not name it.
