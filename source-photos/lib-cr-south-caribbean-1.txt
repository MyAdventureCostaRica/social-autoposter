KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- On 20 December 1872 the ship Lizzie docked at Limón with 123 Jamaican workers for the railway to San José. A year later more than 1,000 Jamaicans were working in the port. [source: https://www.doc.surf/wiki/afro-costa-ricans ; https://jamaicans.com/?p=214641]
- The railway was overseen by Minor Keith, an American who arrived at 23 to help his uncle, Henry Meiggs. [source: https://ticotimes.net/2026/04/19/the-story-of-costa-ricas-famous-railroad-to-limon]
- Until 1949 most Afro-Costa Ricans lived in Limón and were denied citizenship and the vote; the 1949 constitution gave them full rights. Limón Creole is still spoken on this coast. [source: https://Www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Costa_Rican ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lim%C3%B3n_Province]
