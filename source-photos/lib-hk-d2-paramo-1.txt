The Hiking Expedition (Episode 03, hiking) · Day 2 of 9 · Above the treeline. · The páramo
meals: Breakfast · lodge tonight: Lauráceas Lodge, San Gerardo de Dota
The support van climbs the Pan-American Highway into the Macizo del Cerro de la Muerte, the high section of the Cordillera de Talamanca where the Camino Playa El Rey begins. The first day's walk is in the páramo itself — the tropical alpine grassland that in Costa Rica exists only above three thousand metres — among the named summits of the massif, crossing the treeline where the oaks give way to dwarf bamboo and lichen.
In the afternoon you drop into the Dota Valley to Lauráceas Lodge — ten chalets in the cloud forest, a family kitchen with trout and apples from the valley, and, with luck, the resplendent quetzal feeding outside your window in the morning.
Highlight of The Hiking Expedition (Episode 03, hiking): Begin in the páramo. — The first steps are above the treeline, on the spine of the Macizo del Cerro de la Muerte — grassland, dwarf bamboo, and volcanic light at three thousand four hundred metres.
Highlight of The Hiking Expedition (Episode 03, hiking): A sea of clouds. — Dawn from the high ridge, before the descent begins — the Pacific invisible below a white ocean that burns off by nine.
