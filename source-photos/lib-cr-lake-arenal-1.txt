PHOTO: Lake Arenal under heavy cloud, Arenal Volcano on the far shore. Esteban's own photo, March 2024.
PLACE: Lake Arenal and Arenal Volcano, northern Costa Rica, between Tilarán and La Fortuna.
KNOWN FACTS: Lake Arenal is the largest lake in Costa Rica, a reservoir formed by the Arenal dam that feeds hydroelectric plants. Arenal Volcano erupted violently in 1968 and stayed active until 2010. The lake is known for its steady strong winds.
