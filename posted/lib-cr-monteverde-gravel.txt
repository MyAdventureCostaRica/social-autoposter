The Cordillera de Tilarán holds the cloud forest canopy above Monteverde. These gravel roads wind through the range, crossing the terrain that defines the high-altitude riding here.

In 1993, Román Urbina and a group of friends retraced the path of Spanish explorers from the Pacific to the Caribbean. Their ride became La Ruta de los Conquistadores, an event often described as the toughest mountain bike race in the world. In 2016, Urbina was inducted into the Mountain Bike Hall of Fame.

Mountain bike racing in this country demands a specific kind of persistence. The roads here do not flatten, and the weather changes by the hour. That is the reality of riding in the mountains.
