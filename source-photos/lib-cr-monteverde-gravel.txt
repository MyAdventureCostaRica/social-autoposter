KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- La Ruta de los Conquistadores, a 3-day mountain bike race across Costa Rica from the Pacific to the Caribbean, began in 1993: its founder Román Urbina and friends retraced the route of the Spanish explorers. [source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Ruta_de_los_Conquistadores ; https://ticotimes.net/2016/08/04/costa-ricas-roman-urbina-inducted-mountain-biking-hall-fame]
- It is often called the toughest mountain bike race in the world. In 2016 Urbina became the first Latin American in the Mountain Bike Hall of Fame. [source: https://ticotimes.net/2016/08/04/costa-ricas-roman-urbina-inducted-mountain-biking-hall-fame]
- The Cordillera de Tilarán is the mountain range that carries Monteverde's cloud forest. [source: earlier library note]
