PHOTO: A rider climbing a quiet road between dry pastures and windswept trees above Tilarán, early morning. Esteban's own photo, April 2024.
PLACE: The windy ridges above Tilarán, Guanacaste, where the wind farms stand.
KNOWN FACTS: The hills around Tilarán are among the windiest places in Costa Rica, and several of the country's wind farms stand on them. Costa Rica generates almost all of its electricity from renewable sources: water, wind, geothermal and sun.
