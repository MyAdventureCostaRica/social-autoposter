KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- The Templo de la Música, the music pavilion of Parque Morazán, was inaugurated in December 1920. Its architect modelled it on the Temple of Love at Versailles. [source: https://www.nacion.com/el-pais/educacion/quiosco-del-parque-morazan-o-templo-de-la-musica/6W6YSFTJE5GGRHZJAJMGQTAUCM/story/ ; https://www.msj.go.cr/servciud/cult/ChepeAndo/Templo%20de%20la%20Musica%20un%20%20centenario%20albergue%20de%20historias.pdf]
- On 9 August 1884 San José switched on electric street lighting, one of the first cities in the world to do so. The first line ran to La Laguna, the lagoon where Parque Morazán stands today.
