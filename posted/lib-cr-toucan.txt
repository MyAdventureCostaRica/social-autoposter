The keel-billed toucan lives in the northern Pacific lowlands and the Caribbean slope. We ride through its range on the final days of the Gravel Expedition, as the route traces the Guanacaste surf coast north toward Tamarindo.

This is not a bird of the deep south or the high mountains. It is a resident of the dry forest and the coastal corridors where the heat lingers. When you ride out of Nosara toward the finish, look into the canopy.
