Day 7 takes you into the Osa Peninsula for 15.4 kilometres of dense rainforest singletrack. You start in the morning and move through a landscape where the canopy is often louder than your own feet.

Expect 299 metres of climbing and 310 metres of descent. The route winds through river crossings and heavy timber, and the scarlet macaws are resident here in the beach almonds. It is one of the most biodiverse corners of the planet, and the trail is the only way to see it at this speed.

You finish the day at Corcovado Wilderness Lodge, where the jungle meets the Pacific. Because the wildlife here is the reason you run.
