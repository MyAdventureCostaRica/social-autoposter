The Hiking Expedition (Episode 03, hiking) · Day 6 of 9 · The village that stayed. · Mid-valley jungle
meals: Breakfast, lunch, dinner · lodge tonight: Los Campesinos Eco Lodge, Quebrada Arroyo
Downstream the forest thickens into mid-valley jungle, and the trail arrives at Quebrada Arroyo — a village that chose to stay. When the road never came, its families built Los Campesinos Eco Lodge and put more than ninety percent of the community to work in it, protecting thirty-three hectares of their own forest instead of clearing it.
The lodge earns the afternoon: a hanging bridge one hundred and twenty-seven metres long — among the longest in the region — waterfalls with natural pools beneath them, and a working trapiche where sugarcane becomes dulce the way it has for a century. Dinner comes from the village kitchen.
Highlight of The Hiking Expedition (Episode 03, hiking): Across by cable. — The Savegre is crossed the way the mountain families cross it — a hanging bridge and a hand-pulled andarivel in a single day, a bridge a hundred and twenty-seven metres long the day after, cold clean water the whole week.
