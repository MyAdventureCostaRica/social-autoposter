I have spent years scouting the ridges of the Cordillera de Talamanca, and I have learned one thing: the mountain sets the pace. You can plan for every climb, but the humidity, the elevation, and the way the light shifts over the valley will dictate the day's rhythm. When you stop fighting the terrain and start moving with it, the riding changes. It is no longer about the effort; it is about the quiet of the high country.
