PHOTO: A sea-turtle hatchling on the sand of Tortuguero beach at first light, heading for the water. Esteban's own photo, August 2023.
PLACE: Tortuguero beach, north Caribbean coast.
KNOWN FACTS: Tortuguero is the most important nesting beach of the green turtle in the Western Hemisphere; green turtles nest roughly from July to October and the hatchlings run for the sea weeks later. The hatchling's species is not confirmed: call it a sea-turtle hatchling.
The Wildlife Expedition · Day 2 · Into the canals. (Tortuguero) · lodge: Tortuga Lodge & Gardens, Tortuguero · meals: Breakfast, lunch, dinner
A morning flight from San José to Tortuguero, a village on a strip of sand between the Caribbean and the canals, reached only by boat or by plane. The lodge's boat meets the flight. Tortuga Lodge & Gardens stands on the river at Tortuguero, with its gardens on the bank and a saltwater pool — 2 nights here.
In the afternoon, the first boat into Tortuguero National Park: the main canal and the caños that branch off it, a forest that comes down to the water on both sides. In the rainy season the day ends on the beach: the green turtles' nesting walk, between 8 and 10 in the evening, with a licensed local guide, no white light and no cameras — the park's rules, and the right ones.
Bookable departures: 20 – 28 March 2027; 4 – 12 September 2027. Group: 6 to 8 guests. Details and dates in the bio.
