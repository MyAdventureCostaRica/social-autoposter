I spent 15 years learning this massif, and it took 4 attempts before I felt the route was ready for anyone else. The Nueve Cumbres traverse covers 17.3 km along the continental divide, never dipping below 3,000 m. 

I remember the ridge for the days I spent scouting it under rain, at 2 °C. That is not a temperature this country is supposed to allow, and it is the first thing this day takes from you. 

We summit 9 peaks, including Cerro Buenavista at 3,491 m, before the van drops us 1,500 m into the Dota Valley. I stay here because this páramo is the rarest ground in the country, and because the expedition needs athletes to meet the Talamanca on Day 2.
