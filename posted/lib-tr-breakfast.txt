Black beans and day-old rice form the quiet fuel of mountain mornings across Costa Rica.

Cooking gallo pinto requires cold rice so the grains stay separate when tossed with sweet pepper, onion, and fresh coriander. On the Caribbean coast, cooks use red beans and coconut milk instead of black beans.

The Trail Running Expedition traverses 9 days from the 3,000 m spine of Talamanca down through cloud forest to the Pacific for 6 to 8 athletes. August brings clear early starts on the high summits and the Osa Peninsula in peak whale season.
