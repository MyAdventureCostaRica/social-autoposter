The walk begins above the treeline on the Macizo del Cerro de la Muerte, at 3,400 metres.

The ground underfoot is rough singletrack through dwarf bamboo, lichen, and scrub. In Costa Rica, páramo exists only above 3,000 metres, where the mountain spine divides the watersheds.

The work of the morning is the thin air along the ridgeline and the sharp wind that comes with it. After crossing among the named summits, the route leaves the exposed ridge and drops down into the montane oaks of the Savegre drainage toward San Gerardo de Dota.

It is the opening day of the route to the Pacific, and the only one spent entirely in the cold.
