In the Costa Rican highlands, the temperature drops nearly six degrees Celsius for every thousand metres of elevation. Up on these high pastures, the evening air turns cold long before the orange light leaves the sky. Below the ridge, a layer of white mist fills the valleys, leaving only the dark shapes of the distant peaks and the low grass in the foreground.
