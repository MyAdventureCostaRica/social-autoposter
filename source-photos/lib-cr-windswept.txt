KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- Up to 40% of the water the Monteverde cloud forest captures comes from mist, not rain: the trees comb it out of the cloud (horizontal precipitation), even on days when no rain falls. [source: https://www.discoverwildlife.com/environment/monteverde-cloud-forest-preserve]
- The northeast trade winds cross the continental divide at Monteverde, which is why the trees on the ridge grow bent. [source: earlier library note]
