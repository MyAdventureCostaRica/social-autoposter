I have spent 15 years learning this massif, and it took 4 scouting trips to piece the ridgeline together.

The Nueve Cumbres route covers 17.3 km above 3,000 m across the Cerro de la Muerte. Scouting meant days alone on the páramo in cold rain at 2 °C, working out where the trail could link 9 peaks without touching the highway.

From the top, the route drops 1,500 m straight into San Gerardo de Dota, my favourite mountain town in the country. At Lauráceas Lodge, I sit down in the kitchen and ask for hot agua dulce before doing anything else.

We run this high crest on Day 2 because the whole expedition descends from here.
