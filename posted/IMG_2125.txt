Cycling in Costa Rica is at its best when the road climbs out of the valley and lets the wind take over. I look for routes like this when scouting: unhurried elevation, wide-open horizons, and miles of clean track where you rarely meet another soul. This is how we ride the interior — at the pace the terrain demands.
