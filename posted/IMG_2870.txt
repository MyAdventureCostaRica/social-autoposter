Mountain biking in Costa Rica often means riding at the edge of the landscape. Here, the track narrows through the tall, sharp-edged cane grass, opening suddenly to reveal the water below. It is a quiet kind of riding, where the only sound is the wind moving through the reeds and the steady hum of tires on the dirt.
