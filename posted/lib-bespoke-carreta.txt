In 2005, UNESCO added Costa Rica's painted oxcart tradition to its intangible cultural heritage list.

The carreta is the national labour symbol, developed around Sarchí to carry coffee across the cordilleras before paved roads reached the coast. Each painted geometric pattern identified the valley and workshop the cart belonged to.
