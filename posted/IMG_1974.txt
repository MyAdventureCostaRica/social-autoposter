The potoo bird, a master of camouflage, blends seamlessly with its surroundings in Costa Rica's diverse ecosystems. Known for its stillness and nocturnal habits, spotting one in the wild is a rare and magical moment of connection with nature. Worth saving for your next Costa Rica adventure.

Vale la pena guardarlo para su próxima aventura en Costa Rica.
