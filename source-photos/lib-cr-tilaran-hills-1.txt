KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- The Cordillera de Tilarán is the continental divide here: rain on its east side runs to the Caribbean, rain on its west side to the Pacific. The Caribbean slope is far wetter, with more than 7,000 mm a year in places. [source: https://www.redalyc.org/pdf/449/44918952032.pdf]
- Lake Arenal, on the horizon here, is the largest lake in Costa Rica, a reservoir that feeds hydroelectric plants. [source: earlier library note]
