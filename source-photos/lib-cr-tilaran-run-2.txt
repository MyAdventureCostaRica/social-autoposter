PLACE: The Cordillera de Tilarán, Guanacaste.
KNOWN FACTS: The Cordillera de Tilarán is the mountain range that carries Monteverde's cloud forest. The northeast trade winds cross the continental divide at Monteverde; the cloud they carry feeds the cloud forest, which is why the ridges are so often in fog.
