The Guanacaste tree, Enterolobium cyclocarpum, stands in the open cattle country of the northern Pacific. It is the national tree of this country, named for the province where our gravel expedition finds its finish.

On Day 6, we ride north from Nosara toward Tamarindo. The route runs through the surf coast landscapes where these trees provide the only shade for kilometres. It is a fast, celebratory day, with the hardest climbs of the week behind you.
