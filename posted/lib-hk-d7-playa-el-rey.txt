Day 7 of the walking route delivers you from the mountain jungle to the tide line at Playa El Rey.

You begin the week at 3,400 metres in the cold páramo of the Cerro de la Muerte. Over 7 days, the path drops down the Savegre watershed. Montane oak forest yields to mid-valley jungle, then palms, and finally the rocky reef and wild sand between the Naranjo and Savegre river mouths.

No paved road reaches this stretch of coast. The hard part is the cumulative descent from the high divide, but what it gives back is standing in the salt water where the river finishes its run.
