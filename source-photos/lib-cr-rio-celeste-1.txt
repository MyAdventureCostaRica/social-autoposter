SET: rio-celeste
PHOTO: The Río Celeste running turquoise over pale boulders, a yellow footbridge across it, forest on both banks. Esteban's own photo, September 2023.
PLACE: Río Celeste, on the flanks of Tenorio Volcano in northern Costa Rica.
KNOWN FACTS: The Río Celeste rises in Tenorio Volcano National Park. Its colour begins at Los Teñideros, where two clear streams meet: minerals in the water form fine particles that scatter sunlight and turn the river sky-blue.
