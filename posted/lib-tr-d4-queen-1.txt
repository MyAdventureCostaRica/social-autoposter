This is the 25-kilometre descent I have been coming back to for years.

The day opens in Providencia at 1,788 metres. The profile looks like a pure descent, but the trail spends the first 6 kilometres climbing through cloud forest to a high point of 1,910 metres before it lets go. What follows is a controlled fall of more than 1,000 metres through the oak trees.

By kilometre 15, Xinia hands you agua dulce and pineapple empanadas through her kitchen door. At kilometre 21, you pull yourself across the Savegre in a cable basket at 359 metres, right before the final 4-kilometre climb to Ranchos Tinamú.

I built the entire expedition around this single day because it cannot be faked with a support van. It is 25 kilometres earned entirely on foot.
