KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- Lake Arenal, about 85 km2, is the largest lake in Costa Rica, and it is a reservoir. [source: https://ticotimes.net/?p=153620]
- By 1979 the Arenal dam had made the lake 3 times bigger by flooding the valley. The towns of Arenal and Tronadora had to be moved, and 2 new towns were built for their people. [source: https://ticotimes.net/?p=153620]
- Its water feeds hydroelectric plants. [source: earlier library note]
