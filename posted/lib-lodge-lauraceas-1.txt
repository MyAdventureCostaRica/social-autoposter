Lauráceas Lodge sits at 2,200 metres in the San Gerardo de Dota valley.

We sleep here for 2 nights on both our trail running and hiking expeditions, between the high summits and the Savegre trails. Morning starts with the dawn chorus before first light, trout and apples from the valley kitchen, and hummingbirds at the garden flowers. The aguacatillo trees around the chalets are the reason the resplendent quetzal lives in this cloud forest year-round.

6 to 8 athletes per group.

The Trail Running Expedition: 5–13 December 2026, 20–28 February 2027, 7–15 August 2027. The Hiking Expedition: 19–27 June 2027, 4–12 December 2027. Details in the bio.
