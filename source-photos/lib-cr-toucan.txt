KNOWLEDGE photo. A keel-billed toucan. In Costa Rica this species lives on the Caribbean slope and in the northern Pacific lowlands (Guanacaste), not on the Osa Peninsula, so do not place it on the Osa or the Talamanca. Use it for a Costa Rica fact, or for the Gravel Expedition's Guanacaste coast, where this toucan can be seen.
