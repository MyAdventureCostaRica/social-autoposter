KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- Male mantled howlers have an enlarged, hollow hyoid bone near the vocal cords that amplifies their calls. [source: https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Mantled_howler]
- Howler calls carry about 5 km through rainforest. [source: https://ipfs.ssi.eecc.de/ipns/en.wikipedia-on-ipfs.org/wiki/Howler_monkey]
- It is the only Central American monkey that eats large quantities of leaves. [source: https://wikipedia.com/wiki/Mantled_howler]
- Costa Rica has 4 monkey species: the mantled howler, the white-faced capuchin, the Central American spider monkey and the Central American squirrel monkey. [source: earlier library note]
