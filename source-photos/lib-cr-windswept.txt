PHOTO: Trees on the ridge at Monteverde bent and stripped by the wind, blue sky. Esteban's own photo, April 2026.
PLACE: The ridge at Monteverde, on the continental divide.
KNOWN FACTS: The northeast trade winds cross the continental divide at Monteverde; the cloud they carry feeds the cloud forest, which is why the ridges are so often in fog. On the exposed ridges the trees grow bent and low.
