## 1. The Trail Running Expedition  *(Episode 01)*
A nine-day, founder-led luxury trail running expedition across Costa Rica.
- **Route:** Cordillera de Talamanca → Osa Peninsula. Nine summits above 3,000 m; a
  self-supported Queen Stage (Providencia → San Isidro de Dota); cloud-forest
  singletrack; a coastal 10K at Manuel Antonio; a boat into Drake Bay; the Osa.
- **Distance:** ~93 km of running across the expedition.
- **Group:** 6–8 athletes · 2 professional guides · 1 dedicated photographer.
- **Dates:** 5–13 December 2026 (9 days).
- **Price:** **$10,500 single / $9,000 per person double** (ground cost, excl. flights).
- Booked via WeTravel.

## 2. The Mountain Biking Expedition  *(Episode 02)*
A nine-day, founder-led luxury mountain biking tour across the Nicoya Peninsula.
- **Route:** San José → Nicoya Coast. Two hubs — Santa Teresa (3 nights) and
  Tamarindo (3 nights) — linked by a Queen Stage (Lepanto → Montezuma); Mal País to
  Cabo Blanco; Sámara ridgelines; Las Catalinas; a Hacienda Pinilla finale.
- **Distance:** ~293 km riding. **E-bike upgrade on request.**
- **Group:** 6–8 riders · guides · photographer.
- **Dates:** 23–31 January 2027 (9 days).
- **Price:** **$7,900 per person** (ground cost, excl. flights).

## 3. Bespoke Private Journeys
Fully custom trips "designed from a blank page, hand-tested by us, operated
end-to-end" — for travellers with a specific vision (fitness, dates, regions,
comfort, occasion; families, mixed-ability groups, non-active partners welcome).
- **How it works:** enquiry form → 20-minute video call → a transparently costed
  draft itinerary (named lodges, real distances) within 7 days → revise together →
  arrive and do nothing but the experience.
- **Regions:** Central Valley & coffee country, Arenal & Northern Plains, Central
  Pacific (Manuel Antonio), Osa Peninsula & Drake Bay, Nicoya/Guanacaste coast.
- **Lodge tier:** boutique through Aman-tier, to taste.
- Direct line: WhatsApp Esteban.

## 4. School & Educational Programs
Bespoke Costa Rica programs for **international schools, universities, and global
service organisations** — combining service learning, adventure, and cultural
immersion. Every route physically scouted before students arrive; delivered as a
sign-off-ready dossier to the school's Global Service team.
- **Framework:** four evaluation categories (terrain/site, activity, transfer,
  safety) mapped through every itinerary; aligned to a school's own framework if it
  has one.
- **Safety/credentials:** Wilderness First Responder protocols; certified guides
  (WFR, Swiftwater, IRF raft, PADI); registered Costa Rican tour operator with
  civil-liability cover.
- **Delivery:** 2 professional guides + photographer-videographer; daily school
  comms; post-trip debrief and next-year recommendations.
- **Timeline:** ~12–18 months from first conversation to arrival.
- **Example program:** TASIS Costa Rica 2027.
