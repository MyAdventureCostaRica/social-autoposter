When the paved road never reached Quebrada Arroyo, the village made a choice about its forest.

Instead of clearing land for cattle, the local families built Los Campesinos Eco Lodge and protected 33 hectares of mid-valley jungle along the Savegre watershed. Today, more than 90% of the community works directly in the conservation project.

To cross these steep river canyons without clearing canopy, long hanging bridges were built bank to bank—including a 127-metre span suspended above the forest floor.

In this valley, keeping primary forest standing proved more sustainable for the community than cutting it down.
