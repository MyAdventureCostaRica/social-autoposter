KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- Manuel Antonio has both two-toed and three-toed sloths. [source: https://kaweah.freedombox.rocks/kiwix/wikipedia_en_all_maxi_2023-10/Manuel_Antonio_Beach]
- Three-toed sloths come down about once a week to defecate at the foot of their tree. Moths that live in their fur lay eggs in the dung, and the algae that grow in the fur are eaten by the sloth (Pauli et al., 2014, research done in Costa Rica). [source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3906947 ; https://www.livescience.com/42744-why-sloths-leave-trees-to-poop.html]
