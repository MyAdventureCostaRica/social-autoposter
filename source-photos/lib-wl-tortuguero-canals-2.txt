PLACE: Tortuguero's canals and lagoon, north Caribbean coast.
KNOWN FACTS: Tortuguero is a village on a strip of sand between the Caribbean and the canals, reached only by boat or by plane.
The Wildlife Expedition · Day 3 · Dawn on the water. (Tortuguero) · lodge: Tortuga Lodge & Gardens, Tortuguero · meals: Breakfast, lunch, dinner
The water trails open at 6 in the morning, and that is when we are on them: a slow boat on the caños in the hour the forest is loudest, before the heat and before the lodge's breakfast. The rest of the morning is the village — a walk along its one sandy street and the beach the turtles come to.
The afternoon is yours at the lodge: the gardens, the pool, the river going past. In September a second night walk on the beach is possible, if the programme operates that night.
Bookable departures: 20 – 28 March 2027; 4 – 12 September 2027. Group: 6 to 8 guests. Details and dates in the bio.
