Day 6 runs 16.6 km along the Pacific coastal trail from Agujitas deep into the Osa Peninsula.

You start after a boat transit through the Sierpe-Térraba mangroves. The route covers singletrack through the Punta Río Claro National Wildlife Refuge, gaining 285 m and dropping 270 m across stream crossings and hidden coves. The effort comes from humidity and short, repeated climbs along headlands.

It finishes at Corcovado Wilderness Lodge, where the jungle meets the ocean.
