The Hiking Expedition · Day 5 · The river is the trail. (Savegre canyon) · lodge: Refugio del Río Don Hernán · meals: Breakfast, picnic lunch, dinner
Today the expedition meets the river properly. The trail works down into the Savegre canyon and crosses it twice — once on a hanging bridge, once on an andarivel, the hand-pulled cable strung bank to bank that mountain families here have used for generations. Between crossings: pools the colour of bottle glass, walls of primary forest, and the constant company of one of the cleanest rivers in Central America.
The day ends at the Refugio del Río Don Hernán, a family refuge on the riverbank. Nothing about it is polished, and that is the point — falling asleep to the Savegre a few metres from your bed.
