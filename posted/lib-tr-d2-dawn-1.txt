Day 2 begins at 3,000 metres on the spine of the Talamanca range.

You leave the valley in the support van after breakfast, climbing into the páramo of the Macizo del Cerro de la Muerte. What follows is 17.3 kilometres along the ridge: 9 named summits, 1,644 metres of ascent, and a clear morning above the clouds.

By early afternoon the trail ends at Cerro Buenavista at 3,491 metres. From there we drop 1,500 metres into the Dota Valley to sleep at Lauráceas Lodge, where trout and apples from the valley wait in the kitchen.
