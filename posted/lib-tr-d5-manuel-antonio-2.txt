Day 5 trades the high cloud forest for 9.5 kilometres of coastal singletrack at Manuel Antonio.

After 4 days in the Talamanca mountains, the morning run follows the Reto MAE trail from Quepos to the national park gates. The path connects 7 Pacific beaches in sequence, crossing rainforest bluffs over short pitches that total 425 metres of gain at sea level.

The afternoon sits above the water at Hotel Parador, set in 5 hectares of cliff-top rainforest.
