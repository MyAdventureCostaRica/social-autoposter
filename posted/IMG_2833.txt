In the Costa Rican highlands, mountain biking means climbing into a cool, breezy climate far above the coast. Up here, the volcanic soil keeps the pasture grass deep green, framed by a young papaya tree and a horizon of rolling hills. It is a quiet, unhurried world where the only sound is the breeze.
