In the coastal wetlands of Costa Rica, the roads eventually run out and the rivers take over. Travelling by longboat is how you reach the deep forest, where the dark, glassy water reflects a wall of ancient palms and wild ginger. It is a world measured not in kilometres, but in the slow rise and fall of the tide.
