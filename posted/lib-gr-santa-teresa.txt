The end of the first 2 days of The Gravel Expedition. Whether you roll in from the Paquera ferry or descend the forested ridge from Lepanto, the road tips down to this surf coast.

We base here at Hotel Fermata. It is a beachfront boutique on the edge of the Pacific, chosen for the way the kitchen handles dinner after 46.5 km or 55.8 km in the heat. You stay for 2 nights to find your legs and the gravel of the southern Nicoya Peninsula.
