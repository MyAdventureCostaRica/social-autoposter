- Marino Ballena National Park at Uvita protects a sandbar shaped like a whale's tail that appears at low tide. [source: earlier library note]
- Created in 1989, it was Costa Rica's first marine national park. [source: https://www.visitcostarica.com/sites/default/files/legacy/pdf/11_PN%20Marino%20Ballena.pdf]
- Humpback whales from both hemispheres come here to breed: the southern ones roughly from July to November, the northern ones from December to April. [source: https://ticotimes.net/l/whale-economics ; https://www.visitcentroamerica.com/en/?p=4883]
