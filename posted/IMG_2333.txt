Mountain biking in Costa Rica is a constant negotiation with the topography. When I scout these high mountain ridges, I look for the precise point where the wide gravel roads transition into technical, clay-heavy singletrack. Understanding this transition is the key to pacing yourself through climbs that can easily exceed twenty percent gradients. It is a demanding landscape, but it rewards those who know how to read the dirt.
