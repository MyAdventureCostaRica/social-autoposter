PHOTO: A mantled howler monkey resting on a branch in Manuel Antonio. Esteban's own photo, April 2024.
PLACE: Manuel Antonio National Park, central Pacific.
KNOWN FACTS: Costa Rica has 4 monkey species: the mantled howler, the white-faced capuchin, the Central American spider monkey and the Central American squirrel monkey. The howler's call carries far through the forest, most often at dawn.
