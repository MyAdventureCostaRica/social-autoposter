59.2 kilometres north from Nosara bring you to the bay at Tamarindo.

Day 6 runs up the Guanacaste surf coast. From Nosara the route passes Ostional, the fishing cove at San Juanillo, Marbella, Playa Negra at Los Pargos, and Playa Avellanas before the road reaches the edge of Hacienda Pinilla.

It is 59.2 km with 782 m of climbing. With the main interior climbs behind you, the day stays flat and fast along the surf beaches, ending at Hotel Capitán Suizo on the south end of the bay.

Because after 2 days of interior ridgelines and 2,600 m of climbing, the expedition reaches the open ocean.
