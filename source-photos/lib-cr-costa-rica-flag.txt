KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- The flag was adopted on 29 September 1848 at the suggestion of Pacífica Fernández Oreamuno, wife of President José María Castro Madriz. [source: https://www.britannica.com/print/article/1355151]
- Its colours follow the French flag and the ideals of the French Revolution. The red stripe is twice as wide as each of the others. [source: https://crwflags.com/FoTW/FLAGS/cr.html ; https://www.ecured.cu/Bandera_de_Costa_Rica]
- It was first raised on 12 November 1848. [source: https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=110194]
