KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- The tall cypress along many roads here, Cupressus lusitanica, is not native: it was brought in and planted as windbreaks around pastures, and such windbreaks are common around Monteverde. [source: https://digitalcommons.usf.edu/tropical_ecology/254 ; https://dialnet.unirioja.es/descarga/articulo/5123208.pdf]
- The same cypress is the Christmas tree most Costa Ricans buy. [source: https://revistas.tec.ac.cr/index.php/kuru/article/download/478/407/1067]
