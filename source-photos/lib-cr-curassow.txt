PHOTO: A male great curassow, black with a curly crest and a yellow knob on the bill, walking a garden path near La Fortuna. Esteban's own photo, September 2026.
PLACE: A garden near La Fortuna.
KNOWN FACTS: The great curassow is a large ground bird of lowland forest: the male is black with a curly crest and a yellow knob on the bill, the female reddish-brown. Hunting has made it rare outside protected areas.
