A 9.2 km loop from Lauráceas Lodge climbs 496 metres through the montane oak forest above the Dota Valley.

You start at 2,181 metres on damp singletrack along the eastern boundary of Los Quetzales National Park. The trail works upward under mossy robles and aguacatillos to a high point of 2,622 metres before looping back to the valley floor.

The work sits in the climb: nearly 500 metres of steady gain in cool morning air before your legs have warmed.

What the ground gives back is a shaded descent through the wild avocado trees where the resplendent quetzal feeds, dropping you straight back at the lodge before noon.
