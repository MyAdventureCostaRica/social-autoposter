KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- In 1948 four young Quakers from Fairhope, Alabama, were jailed for refusing to register for the U.S. draft. [source: https://encyclopediaofalabama.org/article/quakers-in-alabama/]
- In 1951, 44 settlers, mostly Quaker families from Alabama and Iowa, bought about 3,000 acres of mountain cloud forest here and named it Monteverde, Green Mountain. Costa Rica had just abolished its army, which was part of the draw. [source: https://encyclopediaofalabama.org/article/quakers-in-alabama/ ; https://garysandmanartist.com/costa-rican-friends/]
- They built a cheese factory and set aside the forest above their farms to protect their water. That watershed grew into the Monteverde Cloud Forest Reserve; the Quakers gave a third of the land they had bought. [source: https://garysandmanartist.com/costa-rican-friends/ ; https://www.pbs.org/wnet/religionandethics/2016/06/24/quakers-costa-rica/31089]
- The 44 settlers were mostly Quaker families: do not call all 44 Quakers or pacifists.
