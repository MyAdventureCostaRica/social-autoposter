SET: tarcoles-crocodiles
PHOTO: An American crocodile in the shallow brown water of the Río Tárcoles, seen from above. Esteban's own photo, March 2024.
PLACE: The Río Tárcoles, on the central Pacific coast road.
KNOWN FACTS: American crocodiles gather on the banks of the Río Tárcoles below the bridge on the coastal highway (Route 34), one of the best-known places in Costa Rica to see them. The Tárcoles carries the water of the Central Valley to the Gulf of Nicoya.
