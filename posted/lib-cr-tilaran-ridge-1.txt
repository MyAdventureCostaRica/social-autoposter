Seasons in Costa Rica are named by rain rather than temperature.

The months from May to November are called invierno, or winter, simply because rain falls. Summer, or verano, runs from December to April when the ground dries out.

On the western ridges of the Cordillera de Tilarán, trail conditions follow that wet calendar. Temperatures remain warm throughout the year, but footing on these open grassy slopes changes completely once the rains turn on.

In July or August, a brief break often interrupts the wet months. A few dry weeks suspend the afternoon storms, a window known locally as the veranillo de San Juan, the little summer of Saint John.

Out on these ridges, the conditions for moving on foot are measured in water, not degrees.
