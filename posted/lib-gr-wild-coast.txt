Day 4 of the gravel week runs 101.1 km along the undeveloped middle coast of Nicoya.

You leave Santa Teresa on dirt roads that trace the shore north toward Nosara, accumulating 1,530 m of elevation along the way. The surface is gravel and unpaved ranch road, where river mouths replace bridges.

The challenge is the cumulative distance on open, sunlit stretches past Coyote and San Miguel. What it gives back is a coastline without a highway or resort strips—passing near the turtle grounds at Camaronal and the small village of Punta Islita.

You finish in Nosara having crossed a peninsula on the dirt it was built from.
