Day 5 trades the high Talamanca for 9.5 kilometres of coastal singletrack above the Pacific.

The Reto MAE line runs from Quepos south to Manuel Antonio, crossing 7 beaches in sequence through cliff-top rainforest. The sea-level start is deceptive — short, sharp pitches accumulate 425 metres of gain before the path finishes.

After the run, you recover at Hotel Parador, set in 5 hectares of canopy high above the water.
