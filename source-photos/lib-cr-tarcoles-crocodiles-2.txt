KNOWN FACTS (checked; the source of each is in brackets, for checking only, never for the caption):
- American crocodiles gather on the banks of the Río Tárcoles below the bridge on the coastal highway (Route 34). [source: earlier library note]
- About 2,000 American crocodiles live in the Tárcoles, which a National University biologist calls the most polluted river in Costa Rica: it carries San José's sewage and garbage to the Gulf of Nicoya. [source: https://ticotimes.net/2022/11/25/crocodiles-endure-the-most-polluted-river-in-costa-rica ; https://phys.org/news/2022-11-costa-rica-crocodiles-survive-polluted.pdf]
- They feed on fish that come up the river from the sea, and the country's crocodile population has been recovering since 1980. [source: https://ticotimes.net/2022/11/25/crocodiles-endure-the-most-polluted-river-in-costa-rica ; https://hurriyetdailynews.com/costa-rica-crocodiles-survive-in-most-polluted-river-178936]
