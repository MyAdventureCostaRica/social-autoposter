101.1 kilometres of dirt and river mouths bring you to the Nosara coast.

We ride north out of Santa Teresa through Manzanillo and onto the undeveloped middle coast of Nicoya. This is a 101.1 km traverse past Coyote, San Miguel, and the nesting beaches at Camaronal.

The road is defined by what it lacks: no resort strip, no traffic, and river mouths instead of bridges. You accumulate 1,530 m of climbing before the route drops to the Pacific at Garza and rolls into Nosara.

Tonight we sleep at Sendero Hotel. Tomorrow the Queen Stage climbs 2,632 m straight up into the mountains.

We build this day into the expedition because this coast is the wildest stretch of the peninsula, and the only way to cross it is to ride it in full.
