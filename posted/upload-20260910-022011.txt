Wildlife rehabilitation in Costa Rica relies on sanctuaries that act as bridges between the wild and the recovered. Las Pumas is one of the few places where rescue, research, and release are practiced with long-term intent. It is a quiet reminder that the forest is not just a backdrop, but a community requiring careful stewardship. A stop here often shifts the perspective of an entire journey.
