# Monthly Performance Review: June 2026

## **Readout**
The data indicates that **shares are the live currency** for engagement, accounting for 8.6% of interactions, far outweighing saves (0.3%). ## **Proposed Changes**
1. **Lower REACH_FLOOR to 200**  
   Justification: The top-performing posts in the last year include several with reach between 200–300 (e.g., 272, 298). The current threshold may exclude posts that still provide valuable signals for analysis.

2. **Increase HALFLIFE_DAYS to 120**  
   Justification: Posts from as far back as October 2025 are still contributing meaningful insights. Extending the halflife will ensure we capture longer-term engagement trends.

3. **Remove SAVES_DEAD**  
   Justification: Saves are negligible in the interaction mix (0.3%). Optimizing for this metric would be counterproductive given its lack of impact.

4. **Refine captioner brief to emphasize shareable hooks**  
   Justification: Posts with poetic or emotionally evocative captions (e.g., "It’s not just rain. It’s the sound of Costa Rica calling.") tend to perform well on shares. The brief should encourage opening with a one-line hook that inspires sharing.
