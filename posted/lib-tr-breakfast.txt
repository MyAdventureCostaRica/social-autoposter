Every morning on expedition begins with gallo pinto, fried plantains, avocado, tortillas, and coffee.

Breakfast is included every day on our 9-day routes, served at the lodges before the trail opens. On the Queen Stage through the Dota Valley, 3 or 4 mountain families open their kitchen doors along the 25 kilometres—handing coffee, agua dulce, and empanadas to runners as they pass. At mountain stops like Armonía Ambiental and Los Campesinos, meals come straight from the local gardens.

Every group is 6 to 8 athletes, sharing one long table.
